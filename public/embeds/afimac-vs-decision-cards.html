Direct local hire

Best for

Filling permanent headcount

  • You own recruiting, onboarding, and management
  • Hiring timelines can be too long when the need is urgent

Local staffing agency

Best for

Short-term local gaps and seasonal peaks

  • Agency sources; you still manage the crew
  • When local talent pool is small, hiring is a challenge

AFIMAC Critical Situation Travel Labor

Best for

Critical, time-sensitive gaps local hiring can't fill

  • AFIMAC recruits, travels, houses, and manages the team on site
  • Right answer when the need is urgent and the cost of waiting is high
